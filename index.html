Twofold Weather shows the actual and feels-like temperature in Celsius and Fahrenheit together, so there is nothing to convert. Every reading names its provider and time.

No account, ads, or analytics. Saved places and settings stay on your device. Location is optional.

What it does

Weather you can check at a glance.

  • Up to 12 places

    Search by city name or choose Use my location. Swipe between saved cities, pick a home layout and drag the sections into your own order.

  • Forecasts and details

    Hourly and 10-day forecasts, a 24-hour graph, wind, rain, UV, air quality, pollen in Europe, sun and moon times.

  • Feels like, explained

    See whether wind, humid air or sunshine moves the reading, plus 0 to 10 scores for running, cycling, hiking and more.

  • Rain radar

    Two hours of composite radar from RainViewer, with play, pause and a time slider.

  • Widgets

    Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets show both temperatures in both scales.

  • Forecast alerts

    Set your own thresholds for temperature, rain chance, gusts or UV. Notifications are planned on the device.

Weather data by Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), air quality from CAMS, US observations and alerts from the National Weather Service, and radar by RainViewer.
