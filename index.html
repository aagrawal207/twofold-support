A weather app for iPhone and iPad

Both scales,
side by side.

Twofold Weather shows the actual and feels-like temperature in Celsius and Fahrenheit together, so there is nothing to convert. If you want one scale, Settings can show Celsius or Fahrenheit alone, with one set of units. Old or forecast-based readings are flagged, and the Weather sources page in Settings names each provider and reading time.

What it does

Weather you can check at a glance.

  • Up to 12 places

    Search by city name or use your location, then switch cities with the round button beside the bar. Shape Today and Details your way: drag cards into your own order, move them to the other page or remove them.

  • Today, Week, Briefing and Details

    The current reading, the hours in the colours of their temperatures, 24-hour graphs and the past week on Today; up to ten days on one page; a short briefing on now, this hour, today and this week, with small pictures beside its lines; and on Details one line that sums up the readings and a word in its colour for each: UV index, wind, rain, air quality and more, with pollen in Europe. Tap a reading for its page: the answer first, its scale in colour where it has one, and on most a graph with a key. Every page has a swipe down: tonight's Moon on Today, yesterday on the Week, Activities in the Briefing and the tiles on Details. Swipe up, or tap the section again, to put it away. Sunrise and sunset sit among the hours.

  • Feels like, explained

    See which formula sets feels like and why it feels warmer or cooler, and find a good time for a walk, run, bike ride or hike under Activities in the Briefing.

  • Rain soon

    When rain or snow is close, a next-hour line on Today says when it is due to start or stop. An optional Live Activity shows rain or snow due within two hours on the Lock Screen. Both are estimates from the forecast, not measurements.

  • Widgets

    Eleven Home Screen widgets, thirteen Lock Screen details and an Open Twofold control. The Weather widget shows both temperatures in both scales, or in the one you keep; others cover the next 24 hours, rain in the next 2 hours, the Briefing, Details, feels like, air quality, activities, the past week, the moon and a line of snark. The Apple Watch app shows your cities too.

  • Forecast alerts

    Set your own thresholds for temperature, feels like, rain chance, gusts or UV. Notifications are planned on the device. Official alerts for saved US and Canadian cities are optional.

Weather data by Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), air quality from CAMS, US observations and alerts from the National Weather Service, airport observations from NOAA's Aviation Weather Center, and Canadian alerts from Environment and Climate Change Canada.
